One blackout. One body. Four very good liars.

At 9:17 p.m., the lights went out during an invitation-only soirée at Maison Miette. Six minutes later, the host, Julian Bellamy, was discovered dead on the grand staircase. The body had been moved there.

Four guests were inside. During the blackout, each was in a different room and carried a different object. They did not exchange objects. The culprit used the object they carried.

✧

Your assignment: Work out who killed Julian, with what, and where it happened. Your notebook and evidence are all you need.
